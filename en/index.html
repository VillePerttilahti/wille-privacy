The research behind it

Students learned more than twice as much with an AI tutor

In a randomised controlled trial at Harvard University, students learned more than twice as much in less time with an AI tutor than in an active-learning classroom. They also reported feeling more engaged and motivated.

The trial involved 194 undergraduates on a physics course, and it did not test Will·e. Will·e is built on the same principle: an AI designed to teach, not merely to answer.

Kestin, Miller, Klales, Milbourne & Ponti (2025). AI tutoring outperforms in-class active learning. Scientific Reports 15, 17458.
